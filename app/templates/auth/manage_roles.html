Role management

Assign each account a role. Roles are hierarchical: admin can do everything a maintainer can, and a maintainer can do everything a user can.

  • user — view stats, manage own decks and collection.
  • maintainer — user, plus add players / decks / games and moderate archives.
  • admin — maintainer, plus audit log, Elo recalculation and this role manager.
